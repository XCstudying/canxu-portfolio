## Selected Projects

### 1. Portable MRI Software Product Design
**Role:** Software Product Manager & UI/UX Designer  
**Keywords:** portable MRI, medical workflow, healthcare product design, clinical collaboration, interface design

This project focused on the software system design for a portable MRI product. I participated in the full-cycle product development process, including scenario analysis, user needs identification, workflow planning, interface design, and iterative refinement with engineers and clinical experts.

**What I contributed**
- Conducted scenario analysis and user needs research for portable MRI usage
- Designed workflow logic and UI/UX structure for software functions
- Collaborated with engineers and domain experts to improve product usability
- Supported the translation of complex healthcare requirements into practical product solutions

**Relevance to future research**
This experience strengthened my interest in turning real healthcare needs into computational and system-level research questions, especially in digital health and medical AI settings.

### 2. Elderly Dementia Care Research
**Role:** Research Team Member  
**Keywords:** dementia care, elderly health, community care, user research, healthcare service design

This project explored the needs of elderly users in dementia care contexts through interviews, questionnaires, and service analysis. The work aimed to better understand how home, community, and hospital resources could be connected to support aging populations.

**What I contributed**
- Conducted interviews and questionnaire-based user research
- Analyzed elderly care needs and service gaps
- Participated in building a home-community-hospital collaboration model
- Helped translate user insights into practical healthcare design directions

**Relevance to future research**
This project deepened my interest in aging, healthcare behavior, and human-centered digital health systems.

![Dementia Care Research](assets/dementia_care.png)

### 3. Psychiatric Rehabilitation Interactive Project
**Role:** Team Leader  
**Keywords:** mental health, rehabilitation, serious game, interactive design, Unity

This project was developed in collaboration with a psychiatric hospital and explored how interactive systems could support rehabilitation for stable schizophrenia patients. We designed elements for a serious game experience and investigated user needs in rehabilitation scenarios.

**What I contributed**
- Led user needs research for rehabilitation scenarios
- Identified key user pain points and interaction opportunities
- Participated in interaction design for a serious game system
- Created Unity-based interactive effects to support the project prototype

**Relevance to future research**
This project strengthened my motivation to work on healthcare technologies that combine human factors, interaction design, and data-informed intelligence.

![Psychiatric Rehabilitation Project](assets/psychiatric_rehab.png)

### 4. Diabetic Foot Home Care Research
**Role:** Research Team Member  
**Keywords:** diabetic foot, home care, chronic disease management, user journey, healthcare research

This project investigated the home-care challenges faced by diabetic foot patients. Through narrative interviews and user journey analysis, we identified gaps in knowledge, daily care, and family response capabilities.

**What I contributed**
- Conducted home-care interviews with patients
- Identified key pain points in self-care and family support
- Built personas and user journey maps
- Contributed to the early-stage design direction for home healthcare solutions

**Relevance to future research**
This project expanded my understanding of chronic disease management and the role of human-centered technologies in home-based healthcare.
